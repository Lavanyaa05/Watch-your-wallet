# WYW

WatchYourWallet is a simple and engaging budgeting app designed to help young adults build mindful spending and saving habits. Students and young adults often make frequent small purchases throughout the day, which may seem insignificant individually but quickly accumulate over time. Without consistent tracking, many only realize they have overspent when their bank balance drops unexpectedly.

The app tackles this problem by making expense tracking effortless and intuitive. Its OCR scanner automatically extracts and logs data from receipts, eliminating the need for manual entry. The insights dashboard transforms raw spending data into clear, visual patterns, helping users better understand their habits. Complementing these features is MIRA AI, an intelligent chatbot that provides personalized financial guidance, answers user queries, and encourages smarter spending decisions. Lastly, users can also set monthly budget goals through the profile page, helping them better manage their expenses. Together, these features empower users to stay aware of their finances in real time and take control of their money with confidence.
